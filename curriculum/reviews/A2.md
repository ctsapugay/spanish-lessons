## Findings and fixes

- a2-11, a2-13, a2-14, a2-17, a2-19, a2-22, a2-30: two-speaker exchanges written as one line (*¿Me pasas la sal? Sí, te la paso.*) → marked each turn with the dialogue dash (*—¿Me pasas la sal? —Sí, te la paso.*).
- a2-14: *No tengo ninguna idea* is understandable but not what natives say → replaced with the fixed phrase *No tengo ni idea* in the example and the question, and taught it in the explanation.
- a2-16: *Vaya todo derecho y gire…* uses Spain's *todo derecho* → changed to Latin American *Siga derecho y doble…* in the example, fill-in and word-builder; taught *seguir → siga*, *doblar → doble*.
- a2-28: *Se necesitan camareros* uses Spain's word for waiter → changed to *meseros* (with a note that Spain says *camarero*).
- a2-29: the gustar-type table used *euros* while the course uses Latin American currencies → changed to *dólares*.
- a2-21: "You should rest" accepted only *deberías*; *debes descansar* is also correct → added *debes* to the accepted answers.
- a2-07: the fill-in *___ la luz. (irse, de repente)* was confusing → rewritten as *De repente, ___ la luz. (irse)*.
- a2-22: *estar malo* taught as "ill" without noting that much of Latin America says *estar enfermo* → added the regional note.
- a2-32: *tortilla de papas* could confuse a learner who knows Mexican tortillas → added a note on the two meanings of *tortilla*.

## Checked and found correct (no change)

- All preterite paradigms, including spelling changes (*busqué, llegué, empecé, leyó*), strong stems (*tuve, hice/hizo, dijeron, trajeron*), *-ir* stem changes (*pidió, durmió*), and *ser/ir* (*fui*).
- Imperfect forms and the three irregulars; preterite/imperfect contrasts and the meaning-change verbs (*conocí/conocía, supe/sabía, quise/no quise, pude/podía*).
- Present perfect forms, irregular participles, pronoun placement, and the Latin America vs. Spain usage note.
- Object pronouns (IO, DO, *se lo*), comparatives and superlatives, stressed possessives, ordinals (*primer/tercer*), indefinites/negatives and double negation.
- Formal commands and irregular *tú* commands; *por/para*; periphrases; *hace/desde/llevar*; future and conditional stems; impersonal/passive *se*; gustar-type verbs.
- Regional notes (*camión/guagua/colectivo, boleto/billete/pasaje, ¿Aló?/¿Bueno?/¿Diga?, quedar/juntarse, departamento, lentes/gafas, trapear/fregar*).
