## Findings and fixes

- b1-01: the spelling-change example *coger → coja* uses a verb that is vulgar in much of Latin America → replaced with *escoger → escoja* and added a warning about *coger* (use *tomar / agarrar*).
- b1-09: "about to" was taught as *estar para*, which is Spain's usage; in Latin America it's *estar por* → rewrote the explanation, example, vocabulary and the multiple-choice item (now *por*, with an explanation), and accepted *estar por / a punto de / para* in the translation.
- b1-09: *La casa está por limpiar* ("still to be done") was confusing next to Latin American *estar por* = "about to" → removed.
- b1-34: *Se puso rojo cuando lo vio* was translated "when he saw her" (*lo* = him) → changed to *cuando la vio* in the example and word-builder.
- b1-14: *Si estuviera aquí* was translated "If I were there" → corrected to "here".
- b1-02: *Necesito que quieras ayudar* is grammatical but unnatural → replaced with *Espero que quieras venir.*
- b1-30: the lesson mixed *el azúcar* and *tanta azúcar* (both forms exist, but mixing them confuses) → standardised on masculine: *tanto azúcar*.
- b1-13: two-speaker exchange without dialogue dashes → added dashes.
- b1-18: a nonsense distractor (*ahí mismo aquí*) → replaced with *ahora*.
- b1-27: *Como, …* for giving examples is non-standard → *Como por ejemplo, …*

## Checked and found correct (no change)

- Present subjunctive formation (yo-stem rule, DISHES irregulars, -ir stem changes in *nosotros*) and every subjunctive trigger: wishes, *ojalá*, influence, emotion, impersonal judgments, doubt/denial, *cuando*/time clauses, *para que*, unknown antecedents.
- Indicative for certainty (*creo que, es verdad que, está claro que*), *a lo mejor* + indicative, *decir que* information vs. order.
- Pluperfect, future and conditional of probability, imperfect subjunctive (both -ra and -se), real vs. hypothetical *si* clauses (never conditional after *si*), past triggers → imperfect subjunctive, present perfect subjunctive.
- Reported speech tense shifts and reported questions/commands; reciprocal and accidental *se*; connectors; -mente adverbs and -ísimo; periphrases (*soler, tardar en, llevar sin*); *qué/cuál*; *lo que / lo + adjective*; verbs of change; *vosotros* and *voseo* forms.
