## Lexicon notes

- `Victor` — first name (Victor Hugo) (proper noun).
- `parlâtes` /paʁ.lat/ — passé simple, *vous*, of *parler*; regular form (Bescherelle), too rare to appear in Lexique's corpora.
- `finîtes` /fi.nit/ — passé simple, *vous*, of *finir*; regular form (Bescherelle), absent from Lexique's corpora.
- `prîtes` /pʁit/ — passé simple, *vous*, of *prendre* (Bescherelle), absent from Lexique's corpora.
- `voulûtes` /vu.lyt/ — passé simple, *vous*, of *vouloir* (Bescherelle), absent from Lexique's corpora.
- `anaphore` /a.na.fɔʁ/ (f) — the figure of speech (repetition at the start of successive lines); missing from Lexique, in Le Robert.
- `priori` /pʁi.jɔ.ʁi/ — only in the Latin phrase *a priori* (on the face of it), which Lexique stores as a single two-word entry; the checker reads words one by one.
- `graphique` (m) — *un graphique* (a chart) is masculine (Larousse); Lexique lists the adjective only.
- `présupposé` /pʁe.sy.po.ze/ (m) — noun, *un présupposé* (Larousse, TLFi); past participle of *présupposer*, absent from Lexique's corpora.
