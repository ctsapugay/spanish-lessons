# French C1 — accuracy review

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-17, after all 26 C1 lessons were written. Lessons reviewed: c1-01, c1-02, c1-03, c1-04, c1-05, c1-06, c1-07, c1-08, c1-09, c1-10, c1-11, c1-12, c1-13, c1-14, c1-15, c1-16, c1-17, c1-18, c1-19, c1-20, c1-21, c1-22, c1-23, c1-24, c1-25, c1-26.

## What was checked

1. **Spelling, gender and pronunciation** — `checks/french-accuracy.sh` on every lesson; words
   Lexique lacks (rare passé simple *vous* forms such as *parlâtes*, abbreviations such as
   *PDG*, *CNRS*, *OGM*, regional words such as *natel*, *GSM*, *essencerie*, Roman numerals
   in dates and kings' names, proper nouns) are justified under "Lexicon notes".
2. **Grammar** — passé simple and passé antérieur in narration; inversion after *à peine*,
   *ainsi*, *aussi*, *rarement*, *peut-être*; expletive *ne*; literary negation (*guère*,
   *nullement*, *nul*); concession (*quand bien même* + conditional, *avoir beau*,
   *si… que* + subjunctive, *quel que*, *quoi que*, *où que*); mixed and inverted hypotheses;
   *à ce que* + subjunctive; consequence and cause structures; dislocation and cleft
   sentences; the journalistic conditional; free indirect speech; nominalisation.
3. **Register, rhetoric and DALF C1 method** — the *synthèse de documents*, the *essai
   argumenté* and the oral *exposé* checked against the DALF C1 task descriptions (France
   Éducation international); discourse types, implicit meaning, irony and idioms checked for
   register labels (*casser les pieds* marked familiar).
4. **Facts** — dates and institutions (1789, 1793, 1804, 1871, 1958, the quinquennat, 1981
   abolition of the death penalty, women's first vote in 1945), the French justice system
   (*délit / crime*, *cour d'assises*, *mise en examen*), the francophonie (OIF figures, Belgian
   and Swiss numbers, Québec usage, African French).
5. **Unambiguous items and teaching order** — every multiple-choice and fill-in read again for
   a second right answer; `checks/french-content.sh` for forward references.

## Findings and fixes

- c1-24: an example said women "obtinrent le droit de vote" *au lendemain de la Libération*, but the ordinance granting it dates from April 1944, before the Liberation of Paris → changed to *votèrent pour la première fois* (1945), in the example and the fill-in.
- c1-04: the fill-in *Il est ___ que la situation s'est améliorée* accepted only *incontestable* → *indiscutable* and *indéniable*, equally correct, now accepted.
- c1-22: *Le taux de chômage s'___ à sept pour cent* accepted only *établit* → *élève* (*s'élève à*) also accepted.
- c1-17: *Il laisse ___ qu'il pourrait démissionner* accepted only *entendre* → *penser* also accepted.
- c1-17, c1-18: IPA for words in *-isme* (*euphémisme*, *cynisme*) written /-ism/ → /-izm/ as in Lexique (the *s* is voiced before *m*); *interpeller* written /ɛ̃.tɛʁ.pə.le/ → /ɛ̃.tɛʁ.pɛ.le/.
- c1-18, c1-20, c1-24: fill-in prompts contained English labels ("(a woman)", "(journalistic conditional)", "(historical present)") → moved into the hint.
- c1-21: *la Ve République* used a numeral abbreviation the learner can't check → *la Cinquième République*; *quinquennat* IPA → /kɥɛ̃.ke.na/ as in Lexique.
- c1-22, c1-23: *l'actionnaire* and *l'avocat / l'avocate* hid the gender behind the elided article → *un actionnaire / une actionnaire*, *un avocat / une avocate*.
- c1-26: the Québec affrication was spelled with invented words (*tsu*, *dzi*) → shown in IPA, /tsy/, /dzi/.

## Residual limits

Written and reviewed by an AI, not a native speaker. Regional varieties (c1-26) are taught for
recognition only; usage varies within each country. Figures (number of French speakers,
unemployment rates) are given as rounded examples and date quickly.

## Lexicon notes

- `Victor` — first name (Victor Hugo) (proper noun).
- `parlâtes` /paʁ.lat/ — passé simple, *vous*, of *parler*; regular form (Bescherelle), too rare to appear in Lexique's corpora.
- `finîtes` /fi.nit/ — passé simple, *vous*, of *finir*; regular form (Bescherelle), absent from Lexique's corpora.
- `prîtes` /pʁit/ — passé simple, *vous*, of *prendre* (Bescherelle), absent from Lexique's corpora.
- `voulûtes` /vu.lyt/ — passé simple, *vous*, of *vouloir* (Bescherelle), absent from Lexique's corpora.
- `anaphore` /a.na.fɔʁ/ (f) — the figure of speech (repetition at the start of successive lines); missing from Lexique, in Le Robert.
- `priori` /pʁi.jɔ.ʁi/ — only in the Latin phrase *a priori* (on the face of it), which Lexique stores as a single two-word entry; the checker reads words one by one.
- `graphique` (m) — *un graphique* (a chart) is masculine (Larousse); Lexique lists the adjective only.
- `présupposé` /pʁe.sy.po.ze/ (m) — noun, *un présupposé* (Larousse, TLFi); past participle of *présupposer*, absent from Lexique's corpora.
- `maire` (m/f) — *la maire* is the official feminine for a woman mayor (Guide d'aide à la féminisation; accepted by the Académie française, 2019); Lexique records only the masculine.
- `PDG` /pe.de.ʒe/ (m/f) — abbreviation of *président-directeur général* (CEO).
- `actionnaire` (m/f) — epicene noun, *un actionnaire / une actionnaire* (Larousse); Lexique records no gender.
- `XVI` /sɛz/ — Roman numeral in a king's name (*Louis XVI*, read *Louis Seize*).
- `XVIIIe` /di.zɥi.tjɛm/ — Roman ordinal for centuries (*au XVIIIe siècle*, read *dix-huitième*).
- `Algérie` /al.ʒe.ʁi/ (f) — Algeria (proper noun).
- `Europe` /ø.ʁɔp/ (f) — Europe (proper noun).
- `CNRS` /se.ɛn.ɛʁ.ɛs/ (m) — *Centre national de la recherche scientifique*.
- `doctorant` /dɔk.tɔ.ʁɑ̃/ (m) — PhD student (Larousse); missing from Lexique's corpora.
- `OGM` /o.ʒe.ɛm/ (m) — *organisme génétiquement modifié* (GMO).
- `anglicisme` /ɑ̃.ɡli.sizm/ (m) — an English borrowing (Larousse); missing from Lexique's corpora.
- `multilingue` /myl.ti.lɛ̃ɡ/ — multilingual (Larousse); missing from Lexique's corpora; plural *multilingues*.
- `multilingues` /myl.ti.lɛ̃ɡ/ — plural of *multilingue*.
- `fin` (f) — *la fin* (the end); *la fin de semaine* is the Québec term for the weekend (OQLF).
- `Afrique` /a.fʁik/ (f) — Africa (proper noun).
- `Bruxelles` /bʁy.sɛl/ — Brussels (proper noun); the x is pronounced /s/.
- `Québec` /ke.bɛk/ (m) — Québec (proper noun).
- `Abidjan` /a.bid.ʒɑ̃/ — Abidjan, Côte d'Ivoire (proper noun).
- `GSM` /ʒe.ɛs.ɛm/ (m) — Belgian French for a mobile phone (Larousse, *belgicisme*).
- `natel` /na.tɛl/ (m) — Swiss French for a mobile phone (Larousse, *helvétisme*).
- `essencerie` /e.sɑ̃s.ʁi/ (f) — Senegalese French for a petrol station (Inventaire des particularités lexicales du français en Afrique noire).
- `ambiancer` /ɑ̃.bjɑ̃.se/ — African French: to liven up a party (Larousse, *africanisme*).
