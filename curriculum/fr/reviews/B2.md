## Lexicon notes

- `chais` /ʃɛ/ — the usual written representation of informal *je sais* in *chais pas* (/ʃɛ.pa/), shown only as an example of spoken French.
- `tandis` /tɑ̃.di/ — only in the conjunction *tandis que* (whereas, while), pronounced /tɑ̃.di(s).kə/; Lexique lists *tandis que* only as a unit it doesn't store. In Le Robert and Larousse.
- `Islande` /is.lɑ̃d/ (f) — Iceland, *l'Islande*, *en Islande* (proper noun).
- `argumentée` /aʁ.ɡy.mɑ̃.te/ — feminine of *argumenté* (well-argued), in the DELF task name *prise de position argumentée*; Lexique lacks this form of the participle.
- `responsable` (m/f) — epicene noun: *un / une responsable* (the person in charge); Lexique leaves the gender blank.
- `Obs` — *L'Obs*, a French news weekly (proper noun).
- `Ouest-France` — the most widely read French regional daily (proper noun).
- `INSEE` /in.se/ (m) — *l'INSEE*, the French national statistics institute; an acronym.
- `renouvelables` /ʁə.nu.vlabl/ — plural of *renouvelable* (renewable), as in *les énergies renouvelables*; Lexique lists only the singular.
- `SDF` /ɛs.de.ɛf/ (m/f) — *sans domicile fixe*, a homeless person; an abbreviation.
- `PIB` /pe.i.be/ (m) — *produit intérieur brut*, GDP; an abbreviation.
- `PME` /pe.ɛm.ø/ (f) — *petite ou moyenne entreprise*, an SME; an abbreviation.
- `SMIC` /smik/ (m) — *salaire minimum interprofessionnel de croissance*, the French minimum wage; an acronym said as a word.
- `burn-out` /bœʁ.nawt/ (m) — burnout; an English loan in current French use (*épuisement professionnel* is the French term, also given).
- `fonctionnaire` (m/f) — epicene noun: *un / une fonctionnaire*; Lexique leaves the gender blank.
- `appli` /a.pli/ (f) — short for *application* (an app); informal, newer than Lexique.
- `smartphone` /smaʁt.fɔn/ (m) — English loan in standard French use (*téléphone intelligent* in Québec).
- `IA` /i.a/ (f) — *intelligence artificielle*, AI; an abbreviation.
- `cybersécurité` /si.bɛʁ.se.ky.ʁi.te/ (f) — cybersecurity; newer than Lexique, in Le Robert.
- `cloud` /klawd/ (m) — English loan for cloud computing (*informatique en nuage* is the official French term, also given).
