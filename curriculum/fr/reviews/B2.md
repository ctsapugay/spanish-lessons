# French B2 — accuracy review

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-17, after all 30 B2 lessons were written. Lessons reviewed: b2-01, b2-02, b2-03, b2-04, b2-05, b2-06, b2-07, b2-08, b2-09, b2-10, b2-11, b2-12, b2-13, b2-14, b2-15, b2-16, b2-17, b2-18, b2-19, b2-20, b2-21, b2-22, b2-23, b2-24, b2-25, b2-26, b2-27, b2-28, b2-29, b2-30.

## What was checked

1. **Spelling, gender and pronunciation** — `checks/french-accuracy.sh` on every lesson; words
   Lexique lacks (abbreviations such as *PIB*, *SMIC*, *IA*; modern words such as *appli*,
   *cybersécurité*, *pacser*; proper nouns) are justified under "Lexicon notes".
2. **Grammar** — futur antérieur after *quand / dès que*; conditionnel passé and the
   journalistic conditional; all three *si* patterns and mixed hypotheses; subjunctive after
   doubt, judgement and conjunctions vs infinitive with the same subject; *après que* +
   indicative; past subjunctive; past infinitive (and *ne pas* before the auxiliary);
   present participle vs verbal adjective (*fatiguant / fatigant*); sequence of tenses in
   reported speech; *lequel* and its contractions; past-participle agreement with
   pronominal verbs, *en*, *faire* + infinitive and impersonal verbs.
3. **Register and argument** — formal / standard / informal French described accurately
   (dropped *ne*, *on*, reduced forms); argument and discourse connectors and the French
   *plan dialectique* checked against DELF B2 / DALF expectations.
4. **Facts** — French press titles, the *35 heures*, five weeks' paid holiday, the SMIC,
   *laïcité*, the PACS, the French motto, cultural references.
5. **Unambiguous items and teaching order** — every multiple-choice and fill-in checked for a
   second right answer; `checks/french-content.sh` for forward references.

## Findings and fixes

- b2-08, b2-09, b2-12: invented forms as wrong options (*disé*, *fatiguante*, *convainquants*, *souries*) → replaced by real forms (*fatigue*, *convainquant* — the participle, *souris*).
- b2-22: an option used *Résiliez*, a real imperative but absent from the reference lexicon → replaced by *Annulez tout*, so every option can be checked.
- b2-01, b2-09, b2-23: fill-in prompts contained English labels ("(informal for travaille)", "(tiring)", "(journalistic conditional)") → labels moved to the hint, prompts now French only; b2-01: a French option contained English "or" → *ou*.
- b2-02, b2-16: blanks that made learners type *Lorsqu* / *qu* without the apostrophe → the blank is *Lorsqu'* / *qu'*.
- b2-03: a malformed vocabulary line ("reportedly") with no French or IPA → removed.
- b2-17: capitalised emphasis inside French (*ES-sentiel*) → French written normally, stress described beside it.
- b2-20: *l'animateur / l'animatrice* had one gender for both forms → *un animateur / une animatrice*.
- b2-27: word endings (*-logie*, *-graphie*) in italics, i.e. treated as French words → bold.
- b2-08: *douchée* was tested before being shown → example added.
- b2-13, b2-14, b2-15: pairs of entries with the same English (*sans doute / probablement*, *étant donné que / vu que*, *malgré tout / tout de même*) produced duplicate drills → meanings distinguished (*sans doute* = "probably, not 'without doubt'").

## Residual limits

Written and reviewed by an AI, not a native speaker. Statistics and institutions (minimum
wage, press, political vocabulary) are described as general facts without figures that date
quickly.

## Lexicon notes

- `chais` /ʃɛ/ — the usual written representation of informal *je sais* in *chais pas* (/ʃɛ.pa/), shown only as an example of spoken French.
- `tandis` /tɑ̃.di/ — only in the conjunction *tandis que* (whereas, while), pronounced /tɑ̃.di(s).kə/; Lexique lists *tandis que* only as a unit it doesn't store. In Le Robert and Larousse.
- `Islande` /is.lɑ̃d/ (f) — Iceland, *l'Islande*, *en Islande* (proper noun).
- `argumentée` /aʁ.ɡy.mɑ̃.te/ — feminine of *argumenté* (well-argued), in the DELF task name *prise de position argumentée*; Lexique lacks this form of the participle.
- `responsable` (m/f) — epicene noun: *un / une responsable* (the person in charge); Lexique leaves the gender blank.
- `Obs` — *L'Obs*, a French news weekly (proper noun).
- `Ouest-France` — the most widely read French regional daily (proper noun).
- `INSEE` /in.se/ (m) — *l'INSEE*, the French national statistics institute; an acronym.
- `renouvelables` /ʁə.nu.vlabl/ — plural of *renouvelable* (renewable), as in *les énergies renouvelables*; Lexique lists only the singular.
- `SDF` /ɛs.de.ɛf/ (m/f) — *sans domicile fixe*, a homeless person; an abbreviation.
- `PIB` /pe.i.be/ (m) — *produit intérieur brut*, GDP; an abbreviation.
- `PME` /pe.ɛm.ø/ (f) — *petite ou moyenne entreprise*, an SME; an abbreviation.
- `SMIC` /smik/ (m) — *salaire minimum interprofessionnel de croissance*, the French minimum wage; an acronym said as a word.
- `burn-out` /bœʁ.nawt/ (m) — burnout; an English loan in current French use (*épuisement professionnel* is the French term, also given).
- `fonctionnaire` (m/f) — epicene noun: *un / une fonctionnaire*; Lexique leaves the gender blank.
- `appli` /a.pli/ (f) — short for *application* (an app); informal, newer than Lexique.
- `smartphone` /smaʁt.fɔn/ (m) — English loan in standard French use (*téléphone intelligent* in Québec).
- `IA` /i.a/ (f) — *intelligence artificielle*, AI; an abbreviation.
- `cybersécurité` /si.bɛʁ.se.ky.ʁi.te/ (f) — cybersecurity; newer than Lexique, in Le Robert.
- `cloud` /klawd/ (m) — English loan for cloud computing (*informatique en nuage* is the official French term, also given).
- `Goncourt` — *le prix Goncourt*, France's best-known literary prize (proper noun).
- `Astérix` — the comic-book character (proper noun).
- `œuvre` (m/f) — *une œuvre* (a work — used here) is feminine; *le gros œuvre* (building shell) and the complete works of an artist (*l'œuvre gravé*) can be masculine; Lexique leaves the gender blank.
- `pacser` /pak.se/ — *se pacser*, to enter a PACS (civil partnership, created 1999); newer than Lexique, in Le Robert.
- `mémoire` (m/f) — two nouns: *la mémoire* (memory) and *le mémoire* (a dissertation — used here); Lexique leaves the gender blank.
